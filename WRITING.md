# how i write a note

For me, and for whatever tool is helping me draft. Read this before writing a note for
`_posts/`. The point of a note is that I can re-read it in a year and get the idea back in
thirty seconds.

## the shape

One idea, carried by one picture, broken into short sections.

1. Pick the picture first. A ruler. A door. A shut valve. Something a child could draw.
2. Every section is that same picture seen from another side. If a section cannot be said in
   terms of the picture, the picture is wrong, not the section. Change the picture.
3. Last section is always **why i keep this**. What it changes about my own week. That is the
   part that is mine.

400 to 600 words. If it runs past 600, cut, do not split.

## the voice

- lower case everywhere, including headings and the title
- short sentences, one clause each
- almost no commas, no dashes, no semicolons, no brackets
- second person or first person, never "one" or "we"
- plain words a ten year old reads without stopping. not childish, just unobstructed

## what never goes in

These are the tells. Cut them on sight.

- **"here is why"**, "the thing is", "let me explain", "in this note i will"
- announcing a section before writing it. Write the thing.
- "it is important to note", "it is worth remembering", "at the end of the day"
- three examples where two will do. Lists of three are a habit, not a thought.
- a closing line that tries to be quotable. End on the concrete fact.
- "delve", "crucial", "landscape", "underscores", "highlights", "robust", "leverage"
- em dashes. Full stops instead.
- exclamation marks.

## sources

If a note comes from something I read, the ideas can come across. The words cannot.

- never paste or lightly reword a sentence from the source
- never use a phrase the author coined for their own idea. Say the plain version instead.
- do not name the source unless I decide to. If I do, a link at the foot is enough.
- never attribute an opinion to a named person who did not say it

## the check before posting

- [ ] does one picture run through the whole thing
- [ ] is every heading lower case
- [ ] under 600 words
- [ ] no dashes, no "here is why", no quotable closer
- [ ] does the last section say what changes for me
- [ ] read it out loud. Anywhere I stumble gets cut.

## the file

```
_posts/YYYY-MM-DD-slug.md

---
layout: post
title: "lower case title"
date: YYYY-MM-DD
description: "one line, what it is, for search and for the feed."
---

one line saying what these notes are.

## the picture

...

## why i keep this

...
```

Write it on a phone in the GitHub app or at `github.dev`. Commit. It is live in about a minute.
