notes to myself. about problems i never try to solve.

## the door

a door you walk past every day. you decided long ago it is locked. you have never once pulled the
handle.

that is what i do with my biggest problems.

## it does not feel like a choice

it feels like weather. a fact about the world. not a door at all.

i never weigh it up and decide against it. i flinch before i get that far.

## the biggest doors are the ones i never pull

what i want my life to be. my health. my work. being lonely.

i will fix a small squeaky thing today and leave the big door shut for a year.

## stop trying to open it

i am not trying to get through. i am trying to see what is behind it.

open it is a heavy phrase. look at it is not. and you can start anywhere.

## one hour in the calendar

quiet room. phone somewhere else.

for most of my big doors i have never given ten honest minutes. so i do not know if they are
locked. i only know they feel locked.

an hour is cheap. lose it and i lost an hour. win it and years change.

## what to do in that hour

blank page. write everything you think about it. no standards.

write the problem out as if you were explaining it to someone. the holes show themselves.

imagine it is a month later and the door is open. what happened in between.

ask a friend for the obvious first thing. they say it in five seconds and you have dodged it for
five years.

list ten things you could try. pick one. run it for a week. a failed one still tells you the shape
of the room.

## spotting a shut door

what is the most important problem in my life. why am i not working on it.

no good answer means a shut door.

or imagine a stranger living my life. what would i tell them to go and open.

## some of them really are locked

a few problems are too big for now. that is real.

but you cannot tell from this side. you find out by pulling the handle once.

## why i keep this

my instinct is to work on whatever is in front of me. small things get done. big doors stay shut.

so when i catch myself complaining about something i have never given an hour to i book the hour.
